# Store Screenshots Plan

Six screenshots, all captured from the real app running the seeded demo
account (demo@prayerlock.app), so every pixel is actual product. Device:
6.7 inch frame, light mode, status bar clean (full battery, no carrier
noise). Captions sit above the screenshot in Fraunces, deep green on warm
paper, matching the in app design system.

| # | Screen | Caption (no em dashes, no emoji) |
|---|--------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Today dashboard (score ring, pillar bars, quests) | One score for your whole life |
| 2 | Avatar reveal (Future You) | Meet the person you are building |
| 3 | Quest detail (+XP toast visible) | Small quests, real momentum |
| 4 | Coach chat (answer referencing streak) | A coach who knows your numbers |
| 5 | Insights week view (radar chart) | See the week the way your coach sees it |
| 6 | Achievements (several unlocked) | Proof you are becoming |

Order rationale: screen 1 sells the score mechanic (the hook), screen 2
sells the differentiator (Future Self), screens 3 and 4 prove it works,
screens 5 and 6 prove depth. First two screenshots do most of the
conversion work, so the strongest visuals lead.

Feature graphic (Play Store, 1024x500): wordmark "PrayerLock" in Fraunces
on warm paper, deep green thin ring motif from the score ring, no photo
stock, no gradients.

App icon: the deep green ring with a small gap at the top (a lock that
opens as you progress) on warm paper. Flat, no gloss, no text.

Ramadan variant: swap screenshot 6 for a "30 prayers this month" badge
state and adjust caption to "Ramadan, kept". Prepare 4 weeks before
