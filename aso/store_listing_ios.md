# iOS App Store Listing

## App Name (30 chars max)

```
PrayerLock: Salat and Habits
```
(28 characters. Front loads brand, then the two highest value wedge terms.
"Salat" and "Habits" are indexed, so they are excluded from the keyword
field.)

## Subtitle (30 chars max)

```
Build who you become, daily
```
(27 characters. Identity promise, no keyword stuffing, reads like a human
wrote it.)

## Keyword field (100 chars, comma separated, no spaces)

```
tracker,muslim,deen,salah,streak,quran,future,self,discipline,iman,coach,daily,habit,pray,faith
```

95 characters. Rules honored: no duplicates of title words (Apple indexes
PrayerLock, Salat, and Habits from the name for free), variant spellings
covered (salah alongside salat in the title), no competitor trademarks.

## Promotional text (170 chars, editable without review)

```
Your Future Self is waiting. Daily quests across prayer, health, family,
work, and rest. One score, one streak, one coach who knows your goals.
```

## Description

```
PrayerLock answers one question: who are you becoming?

Most apps track what you do. PrayerLock tracks who you are becoming. Set
your goals across five pillars of life, Health, Family, Work, Fun, and
Spirituality, and meet the person you are building toward.

YOUR FUTURE SELF
During onboarding, PrayerLock creates your Future Self, a coach who knows
your goals, your progress, and your streaks. Ask for advice, get honest
answers that reference your real numbers, and stay accountable without
being judged.

DAILY QUESTS
Every morning you get 10 to 15 quests across your five pillars. Prayer
times, workouts, deep work, family calls, and rest, balanced so one area
of life never eats the others. Complete a quest, earn XP, keep the streak.

ONE SCORE
Your Daily Life Score, 0 to 100, sums up the day across all five pillars.
Watch it rise as you stay consistent. Weekly reports show trends, patterns
you missed, and a forecast for next week.

GROWTH THAT COMPOUNDS
Levels, titles, and badges mark the path from Novice to Legendary. Thirty
prayers in a month, a perfect day, a 7 day streak, each milestone is
recognized.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your coach runs with your data, on your account, never sold or shared.
Export everything or delete everything, anytime, from your profile.

PrayerLock is for anyone building discipline with faith at the center.
Start today. Your Future Self is waiting.
```

## What is deliberately absent (and why)

- No screenshots of fake five star reviews (against store policy and
  instantly recognizable as low effort).
- No "revolutionary" or "best app" claims. Reviewers and users discount
  them; the stores penalize keyword stuffing.
- No pricing tier tables in screenshots. v1 has no paywall.
